Yes — cash on delivery is our only payment method for now. Place the order, and pay the courier when your parcel arrives. Nothing is charged online.

Unopened products can be returned within 7 days of delivery. If a parcel arrives damaged or incorrect, send us a photo on WhatsApp within 48 hours and we'll replace it at our cost.

Are the products authentic and safe?+

Every unit is filled and sealed for Iconix Beauty and sold only here — never through a third party. The formulas are cruelty free, non toxic and dermatologically recommended. Full ingredient lists are printed on every carton and listed on each product page.

Will the tinted sunblock match my skin?+

The tint is a single sheer, warm-neutral shade designed to adapt across medium to deep tones rather than mask them. It evens out redness and pigmentation without leaving a white cast.
