Iconix Beauty — GitHub Pages deployment

1. Create a new repository on GitHub (e.g. iconix-store).
2. Upload EVERYTHING INSIDE this folder to the repository root — the files
   themselves, not the folder. index.html must sit at the top level.
   Pages: index.html (home), shop.html, product.html, checkout.html.
   SiteHeader.dc.html and SiteFooter.dc.html are shared partials — keep
   their names exactly as they are.
3. Repo → Settings → Pages → Source: "Deploy from a branch",
   Branch: main, Folder: / (root) → Save.
4. Wait ~1 minute. Your site is live at
   https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/

Before you go live, open store.js and fill in:
  - orderEmail   (your order inbox)
  - whatsapp     (923XXXXXXXXX)
  - the three emailjs ids  (see SETUP.md, 5 minutes, free)

Until the EmailJS ids are filled in, checkout still works — it hands the
customer a one-tap "send on WhatsApp / send by email" with the full order.

Custom domain: Settings → Pages → Custom domain, then add a CNAME record
at your registrar pointing to <your-username>.github.io.

IMPORTANT — upload ALL 8 files at the root, including the two with
capital letters. If SiteHeader.dc.html or SiteFooter.dc.html are missing,
the top of the page shows an empty grey band instead of the menu.

Root must contain:
  index.html
  shop.html
  product.html
  checkout.html
  SiteHeader.dc.html      <-- the menu + shopping bag
  SiteFooter.dc.html      <-- the footer
  store.js
  support.js
  .nojekyll
  assets/                 (folder with all the images)
