Iconix Beauty — GitHub Pages deployment

1. Create a new repository on GitHub (e.g. iconix-store).
2. Upload EVERYTHING inside this folder to the repository root
   (index.html must sit at the top level, not inside a subfolder).
3. Repo → Settings → Pages → Source: "Deploy from a branch",
   Branch: main, Folder: / (root) → Save.
4. Wait ~1 minute. Your site is live at
   https://<your-username>.github.io/<repo-name>/

Before you go live, open store.js and fill in:
  - orderEmail   (your order inbox)
  - whatsapp     (923XXXXXXXXX)
  - the three emailjs ids  (see SETUP.md, 5 minutes, free)

Until the EmailJS ids are filled in, checkout still works — it hands the
customer a one-tap "send on WhatsApp / send by email" with the full order.

Custom domain: Settings → Pages → Custom domain, then add a CNAME record
at your registrar pointing to <your-username>.github.io.
