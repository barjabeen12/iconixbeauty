Privacy Policy — Iconix Beauty

Privacy policy

Your information, kept simple.

What we collect

When you place an order we collect your name, phone number, delivery address, province, city, and optionally your email — nothing more. We do not collect payment details, because payment is cash on delivery.

How we use it

Your details are used only to deliver your order and to contact you about it on WhatsApp. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone except the courier delivering your parcel.

Where it lives

Orders reach us by email. Your shopping bag is stored only in your own browser. We don't run tracking or advertising cookies on this site.

Your choices

Want your details removed after delivery? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll delete the order record.
